    For Sarah

    Start here

    Sarah, this website is yours. Its files, its history and its address are in a repository you control, and they can move anywhere at any time. This page shows you how to take it over.

    1. See your site

    Your site is at sarah-cruz.designer.sovereignweb.site. Use Choose a design at the top to compare the five designs, and tell us which one you want as the home page.

    2. Join the SovereignWebsite organisation on GitHub

    1. If you do not have a GitHub account yet, make one at github.com/signup and send Dinis your username.
    2. Dinis invites you to the SovereignWebsite organisation as a Member and gives you write access to your site's repository. You need both, or Claude's GitHub check will fail.
    3. Accept the invitation at github.com/orgs/SovereignWebsite/invitation. The link in the invitation email may not offer an accept button, so use this one.

    3. Connect Claude to GitHub

    1. Open claude.ai, go to Settings, then Connectors, and connect GitHub with the same account.
    2. If Claude says it cannot see the repository, press "Check again" once you have accepted the invitation.
    3. Start a new session at claude.ai/code and pick SovereignWebsite/Site__Designer__Sarah-Cruz.

    4. Paste this message

    I am Sarah Cruz and this repository is my portfolio website, published at https://sarah-cruz.designer.sovereignweb.site. Please read CLAUDE.md, README.md and docs/handover.md first. Projects are in tools/work.json, the other words in tools/designs.py, the looks in site/css/ and site/js/, and you rebuild every page with python3 tools/designs.py. Only use facts I give you: never invent a client, a figure or a quote, and credit every photograph. Use plain British English with no em dashes. Keep the site static, with no cookies or tracking. Push to the dev branch only; that publishes the site. Show me what you plan to change before you push.

What I would like to change: (write it here)

    5. Things you might ask for

    • "Use The Wine List as my home page."
    • "Add these four new pet portraits." (attach the photos)
    • "Add my email address to the contact section."
    • "Remove the Butternut Box concept."

    Open questions for you

    A few things we left out until you say: see questions for Sarah. The full handover note is docs/handover.md. You do not need to sign commits.
