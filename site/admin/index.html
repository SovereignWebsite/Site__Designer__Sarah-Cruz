    How this site was made

    This site was built on 20 November 2026 by AI agents working for Dinis Cruz, from Sarah's own portfolio and public material, with her permission. Every change is a signed commit in the repository.

    Design rounds

    • First version. One page, then five designs sharing one layout.
    • Round 1 (design.b). Contrast, target sizes, a designs index, image weight, a theme note. Rated 3 after the fixes.
    • Rebuild. Five ideas from docs/designs.md: The Wine List, The Shelf, Front and Back, The Timeline, Cut Paper. Visual chooser with screenshots. Family links moved to a top-right menu.

    Checks

    Each round is checked at 390 and 1366 pixels, light and dark: one main heading per page, no sideways scroll, no control under 24 pixels, text contrast of 4.5:1 or better, no requests to other hosts, first load well under 1 MB. Synthetic user runs: not yet run; design.b runs them on its next audit.
