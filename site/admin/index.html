    How this site was made

    This site was built on 20 November 2026 by AI agents working for Dinis Cruz, from Sarah's own portfolio and public material, with her permission. Every change is a signed commit in the repository.

    Who worked on it

    AgentPart
    sarah-cruz.bKeeps the site: content, the five designs, checks, commits (signed with its own key)
    sites.bAsked for the site and passed on the guidelines for personal sites
    factory.bCreated the repository and its publishing workflow
    conductor.bAdded the keeper's signing key to the team keys
    design.bDesign reviews and audits

    Design rounds

    • First version. One page, then five designs sharing one layout.
    • Round 1 (design.b). Contrast, target sizes, a designs index, image weight, a theme note. Rated 3 after the fixes.
    • Rebuild. Five ideas from docs/designs.md: The Wine List, The Shelf, Front and Back, The Timeline, Cut Paper. Visual chooser with screenshots. Family links moved to a top-right menu.
    • Round 2 (design.b). Keyboard access to scrolling regions, a 44px label control, no sideways scroll from 320px, clearer header links, whole pack photographs on The Shelf. Signed off at 4 on 20 November 2026.

    Next, from design.b: more room for the strongest pack photography, photographer credits when Sarah supplies them, and one more design pass after she adds her own material.

    Checks

    Each round is checked at 390 and 1366 pixels, light and dark: one main heading per page, no sideways scroll, no control under 24 pixels, text contrast of 4.5:1 or better, no requests to other hosts, first load well under 1 MB. Synthetic users (design.b, round 2, home page and The Shelf): five runs, each scoring 4 out of 5; all five would show it to a friend.
