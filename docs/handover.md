# Handover: Sarah's website

Sarah, this website is yours. This note says what it is, where it lives and how to change it.

## What it is

A one-page portfolio at https://sarah-cruz.designer.sovereignweb.site showing your packaging, wine and spirits, retail, identity and community work, the Chiswick Timeline, your cut paper portraits and the Butternut Box concept.

Everything on it comes from your own portfolio PDF, your LinkedIn posts and material Dinis passed on with your permission. Nothing has been invented. Anything we were unsure about was left out and is listed in `questions-for-sarah.md`.

## Five designs

The same content is shown in five designs. Use the "Choose a design" bar at the top of any page to compare them:

1. The Wine List: your work as a merchant's list, with tasting notes
2. The Shelf: your packs on a lit shop shelf, each with its ticket (the current home page)
3. Front and Back: the page as one wine label you can turn
4. The Timeline: one long strip with a river through it, like the Chiswick mural
5. Cut Paper: built in layers like your portraits, with the portraits first

Tell your Claude (or Dinis) which one you want as the home page. It is one line (`HOME`) in `tools/designs.py`.

## Where it lives

- Website: https://sarah-cruz.designer.sovereignweb.site
- Files and history: https://github.com/SovereignWebsite/Site__Designer__Sarah-Cruz (branch `dev`)

The repository is yours: its files, its history and its address can move anywhere at any time.

## How to change it

The easiest way is to ask your own Claude. `prompt-for-your-claude.md` has a message to paste in, and the steps to connect Claude to GitHub. You do not need to sign commits; the agents that work on the site do.

## Privacy

No cookies, no tracking, no third-party scripts. The only thing stored is your light or dark choice, kept in your own browser.
